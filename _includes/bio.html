Physical AI · Simulation · Synthetic Data

I am a Principal Scientist in Physical AI Research and Director of Simulation Ecosystem Development at NVIDIA.

Research
I lead global research and engineering efforts to build the platforms, workflows, and infrastructure that enable intelligent systems to learn and operate in the physical world. My work connects research to products, with a focus on simulation, synthetic data, and the training and validation of capable, safe autonomous systems.
Open source
Through foundational work on SYNTHIA and CARLA, I have helped advance the use of synthetic data and simulation for autonomous driving and Physical AI. I lead the CARLA organization and have helped grow open-source communities around CARLA and Open3D. As a strong open-source advocate and co-founder of the Physical AI Foundation, I work to make Physical AI technologies accessible to researchers, developers, and industry.
Background
Before joining NVIDIA, I directed the Autonomous Agents Lab at Intel Labs, and served as Principal Investigator for DARPA RACER-SIM, leading an international consortium developing simulation technologies for off-road autonomy. Earlier, I was a Research Scientist at the Toyota Research Institute, working on computer vision, sensor simulation, and sim-to-real for autonomous driving. I earned my PhD in Computer Vision, cum laude, from the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and the Computer Vision Center in 2016.
